07 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Here's what you should know before applying.

Who can apply?+

Anyone aged 21 or older can apply — freshers and experienced professionals are equally welcome. A valid passport is required for overseas employment consideration.

When and where will the events take place?+

Auditions run simultaneously in all 20 cities from 18/01/2027 to 25/01/2027. Venue addresses and appointment times will be shared by email and WhatsApp about seven days before the event.

What will happen at the audition?+

There will be one profession-specific practical round. For example, models may demonstrate posing and movement, designers may present creative work, camera professionals may show production skills, and beauticians may undertake a relevant practical evaluation.

Will I receive a result immediately?+

Selections may be made during the event itself, and we aim to share results within three days. Being shortlisted is not a guarantee of a job offer.

What does ₹850 cover? How do I pay?+

The ₹850 event-service charge covers venue access, an audition session, water, unlimited refreshments and an assessment kit for beautician candidates. You pay it securely via Razorpay at the end of the application form, using UPI, a card, net banking or a wallet. No fee buys employment, selection or a visa.

What is the refund policy?+

If Belle N Beast cancels the event, the refund is initiated and the full ₹850 is returned to your original payment method within 7 days. If you cancel, no refund is given. See the full refund policy.

What salary will I receive in Dubai?+

Salary and vacancies vary by profession. Your salary, benefits, employment conditions and relocation support will be set out in your individual formal offer. Audition attendance does not guarantee employment.

Will Belle N Beast arrange travel and accommodation?+

Yes. For selected full-time employees, Belle N Beast arranges the visa, flight, accommodation, transport and medical insurance. The exact terms are confirmed in your formal offer and employment documents.

When do I get my registration ID?+

Immediately after your ₹850 payment is confirmed. It appears on screen (with copy and print options) and stays available if you reopen the application on the same device. Keep it safe — you need it at check-in. Your city, date and time of day are preferences; exact venue and appointment time are shared about seven days before the event.
