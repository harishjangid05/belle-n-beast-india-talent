Refund policy.

This policy covers the ₹850 event-service fee paid when registering for the Belle N Beast India Talent Hunt 2027 (18/01/2027 – 25/01/2027).

If Belle N Beast cancels the event Full refund in 7 days

Refund of the full ₹850 is initiated and completed within 7 days.

If you cancel No refund

The fee is non-refundable once you cancel your registration.

If the event is cancelled by Belle N Beast

If Belle N Beast cancels the event, we will initiate a refund within 7 days of the cancellation, and the full ₹850 will be refunded within those 7 days. You do not need to apply for it — the refund is processed automatically against your registration ID.

If you cancel your registration

If you decide to cancel your registration or do not attend the event, no refund will be given.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are made through Razorpay to the original payment method used at registration (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). We will notify you by email and WhatsApp when your refund is initiated. Depending on your bank, the amount may take a few extra working days to appear in your account after it is processed.

Important

Paying the event-service fee does not guarantee selection, employment, a salary or a visa.
