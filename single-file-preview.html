08 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Here's what you should know before applying.

Who can apply?+

Applicants aged 21 or older, including freshers and experienced professionals, may express interest. A valid passport is required for overseas employment consideration. Additional role-specific eligibility and final verification requirements: TBD.

When and where will the events take place?+

All 20 cities are scheduled for 1–7 January 2027 simultaneously. Exact venue addresses and appointment hours are TBD. The organiser intends to publish venues approximately seven days before the event.

What will happen at the audition?+

There will be one profession-specific practical round. For example, models may demonstrate posing and movement, designers may present creative work, camera professionals may show production skills, and beauticians may undertake a relevant practical evaluation. Exact assessment criteria: TBD.

Will I receive a result immediately?+

Selections may be made during the event. The organiser is targeting result communication within three days. This is a target, not a guarantee of a job offer.

What does ₹850 cover? Can I pay now?+

The proposed ₹850 event-service charge covers venue access, an audition session, water, unlimited refreshments and an assessment kit for beautician candidates. Payments are currently disabled. Final legal compliance, payment arrangements and service terms must be approved before activation. No fee buys employment, selection or a visa.

What is the refund policy?+

The organiser proposes no refund for candidate-initiated cancellations and a full refund to the original payment source within seven days if the company cancels the event. These are draft terms subject to applicable law and final review; no payment is being accepted on this demo site.

What salary will I receive in Dubai?+

Salary and vacancies vary by profession and are TBD. Any actual salary, benefits, employment conditions and relocation support will be detailed in an individual formal offer. Audition attendance does not guarantee employment.

Will Belle N Beast arrange travel and accommodation?+

The organiser states that it will arrange visa, flight, accommodation, transport and medical insurance for selected full-time employees. Exact eligibility, scope and contract terms are TBD and must be communicated through a formal offer and applicable employment documents.

Does the form submit an application or reserve a slot?+

Not in this frontend release. The form only demonstrates the future application journey. Backend storage, real-time slot capacity, payment, QR confirmation, email and WhatsApp are not connected.
